ASTRO SIEGE

Astro Siege: an arcade shooter with a sci-fi story told between stages, in an endless loop that only grows more difficult. Choose your ship in the hangar; each one has different speed, fire rate, damage and life, and unlocks with coins you earn by playing. Drag your finger to move in any direction, or use the arrow keys. Your ship fires automatically. Collect colored power-ups and defeat the guardians of each sector.
